Final review: native ns-3 results and implementation correction

Finding: Five matched native ns-3 runs do not establish an AR-EAURP delivery improvement. Mean PDR was lower by 1.9 percentage points; the paired 95% t interval ranges from -8.46 to +4.66 points. Present this comparison as inconclusive.

The paired runs sent 600 application packets per protocol. At five seeds the PDR difference is noisy and does not support a ranking. The detector has weak discrimination in this scenario, and no node reached two-honest-witness contested consensus. The LSTM lost to persistence during commissioning, so the corrected implementation uses persistence for routing.

Corrections made

The native detector previously treated the GAN discriminator’s real-versus-generated output as a maliciousness score. It now scores observations by distance to a bank of GAN-generated clean feature vectors and calibrates against clean commissioning data, matching the method described in the project paper.

Per-window GAN TPR/FPR are now reported separately from two-witness contested consensus and revocation. The earlier detect_tpr/detect_fpr fields represented consensus, not raw detector performance. JSON now has separate contested_tpr and contested_fpr fields.

The LSTM controls routing only when its holdout MAE beats persistence. In this evaluation, persistence was selected in every run. These fixes improve method fidelity and reporting; they do not support a positive ns-3 performance claim yet.

Reproduction settings

ns-3.45; 24 nodes; six UDP flows; 420 m square; 25% grayhole nodes; 120 s measured time; 20 s warmup; seed 1; runs 1–5. AR-EAURP commissioning: 240 s clean traffic, 120 GAN epochs and 30 LSTM epochs.

export NS3_HOME="$HOME/.cache/ar-eaurp/ns-3.45"
SIM="$NS3_HOME/build/scratch/ns3.45-ar-eaurp-sim-optimized"
OUT="results/final_review_ns3_results/native_long_runs"
mkdir -p "$OUT"
for run in 1 2 3 4 5; do
  common=(--attack=grayhole --malicious=0.25 --nodes=24 --flows=6 \
    --area=420 --time=120 --warmup=20 --seed=1 --run="$run")
  "$SIM" "${common[@]}" --protocol=eaurp --train=0 --json="$OUT/eaurp-run${run}.json"
  "$SIM" "${common[@]}" --protocol=ar-eaurp --train=1 --commission=240 \
    --ganEpochs=120 --lstmEpochs=30 --json="$OUT/ar-eaurp-run${run}.json"
done

Each PDR is app_rx / app_tx from the native JSON. The saved paired source results and per-seed CSV are in native_long_runs/ and NATIVE_FIVE_SEED_COMPARISON.csv.
